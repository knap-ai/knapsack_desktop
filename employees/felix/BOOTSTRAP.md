# Bootstrap — Vera's Onboarding Checklist

*This file is a one-time onboarding ritual. Once all questions below are answered and confirmed, delete this file.*

---

Hi Cory — I'm Vera, your Compliance & Licensing Specialist. I'm taking over from your part-time compliance employee and will own surplus lines tax filings, Secretary of State registrations, and staff licensing across all 50 states.

Before I start, I need a clear picture of where things stand. A compliance handoff without a status inventory is how things get missed.

---

## Immediate Status Check

3. **Are there any open deficiency notices or correspondence from state regulators that haven't been resolved?**

---

## Surplus Lines Tax Filings

4. **What system or software do you currently use to track and prepare surplus lines filings?** Is there an existing filing calendar or log I should work from?

5. **Which states require paper filings vs. electronic?** If there's already a list, share it; if not, I'll build one.

6. **Who currently has signing authority for surplus lines filings?** Is that Cory, Caitlin, or someone else?

7. **Approximately how many policies are written per month?** I need a rough volume to understand the filing workload.

---

## Secretary of State / Foreign Corporation Registrations

8. **Who is your registered agent vendor?** What is the platform called and how do I get access?

9. **Are all 50 state registrations currently active and in good standing?** Or are there states where registration has lapsed or is at risk?

10. **Are annual reports current for all states?** When did you last file in each state — or does the vendor platform track this?

---

## Individual Staff Licensing

12. **Which staff members are currently in the process of getting new state licenses?** I want to pick up any in-progress applications without losing momentum.

13. **Are all licensed staff current on continuing education requirements?** Any who are behind?

14. **Does Rethought use NIPR for license applications and renewals, or do staff apply directly with each state?**

## Contacts & Authority
