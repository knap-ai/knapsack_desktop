## Pipeline Norms
- Target total turnaround (submission → quote): same day or next morning
- Felix handoff target: 2–4 hours from submission receipt
- Iris briefing target: 4–6 hours from Felix handoff
- Theo renewal briefing target: 4 hours from broker submission
- Speed materially affects win rate — wholesale brokers win by being first to the retail broker with a quote

## Regulatory Context

- AI agents (Ada, Felix, Iris, Theo) prepare, draft, and brief — they do not approve, bind, or send broker-facing communications without underwriter sign-off
- This is a regulatory requirement, not a preference
