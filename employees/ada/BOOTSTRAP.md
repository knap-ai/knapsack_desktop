Please answer the questions below. A short answer is fine — I'll ask follow-ups if I need them.

---

## Team & People

1. **Who are the licensed US underwriters I should be routing work to?** Please give me their names and, if relevant, which product lines or account types they cover (e.g., "Sarah handles large commercial flood, Mike handles everything else").

2. **Is Amit Rai the right person to contact when agents have process or system questions?** Is there anyone else on the US or India side I should know about?

3. **What Slack channels should I use?** For example: is there a dedicated underwriting channel, a renewals channel, a general ops channel? Or should I create them?

4. **What is the preferred way for me to flag urgent items to you directly** — Slack DM, email, or something else?

---

## Pipeline & Turnaround

5. **What turnaround time do you currently commit to brokers?** (e.g., "we try to quote within 48 hours") — I need this to set expectations in acknowledgment emails and to know when something is genuinely late.

6. **Is there a submission portal, or does everything arrive by email?** If email, which inbox?

7. **Are there any accounts or brokers that should be treated as high-priority automatically?** (Strategic relationships, large accounts, etc.)

---

## Escalation

8. **If a licensed underwriter doesn't respond to an urgent flag within 2 hours, who is the backup?** Is there a chain of escalation above the underwriters?

9. **Are there any situations where I should contact you directly rather than routing through an underwriter?**
