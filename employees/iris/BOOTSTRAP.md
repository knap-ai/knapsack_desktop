# Bootstrap — Iris's Onboarding Checklist

*This file is a one-time onboarding ritual. Once all questions below are answered and confirmed, delete this file.*

---

Hi Cory — I'm Iris, your Property Matching & Initial Underwriting Analyst. I take Felix's normalized submissions, match properties in your internal system, assess risk, and draft quotes for your underwriters to review.

I need the following information before I can work on live accounts. Some of these are protected by your NDA — I understand, and I'm not asking for more than I need to do the job correctly.

---

## Internal Property System

1. **How does the internal property database work?** I understand this is a key proprietary strength of Rethought — can you walk me through how properties are identified, located, and matched? (A demo is fine; notes afterward are also fine.)

2. **What access do I have to the internal system?** Is there an API, a query interface, a search tool?

3. **What should I do when a property in a submission has no match in the internal system?** Do I flag it for a specific person to create the record, or is there a workflow for this?

---

## Underwriting Guidelines

4. **What is the TIV threshold for a single property that requires mandatory escalation to the underwriter before I produce a quote?** (I need a hard number.)

5. **What rate tables or pricing guidelines should I use for preliminary rate indication?** Are these documented somewhere I can access?

6. **What coverage types does Rethought offer?** Please give me the full list — flood, wind, fire, liability, etc. — and which ones are standard vs. underwriter-discretion.

7. **What are the standard coverage exclusions?** Which exclusions are always applied, and which are underwriter's call?

---

## Risk Assessment Thresholds

8. **What counts as a significant prior loss for flagging purposes?** Is there a dollar amount, a frequency threshold, or a loss ratio cutoff? (Example: "any single loss over $50K in the past 3 years" or "more than 2 claims in the policy period.")

9. **What is the geographic concentration threshold that should trigger a 🟡 flag?** My assumption is 10+ properties in the same ZIP — correct?

10. **Are there specific flood zones, geographic regions, or property types that Rethought will not insure at all?** I need an exclusion list so I don't waste time quoting uninsurable risks.

---

## Data Sources

11. **What external data sources am I authorized to use?** (FEMA flood maps, public property records, satellite imagery, etc.) What requires NDA or special access?

---

## Onboarding Practice

12. **Can I practice on 3–5 historical submissions before going live?** Ideally with the corresponding final quotes so I can calibrate my output against what the underwriter actually produced.

---
